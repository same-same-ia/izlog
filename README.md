# izlog

izlog je ploča hiragane koja pjeva: dodir na slog pušta ga otpjevanog na njegovoj visini, a
upisani slogovi postaju kratka melodija s notnim zapisom. Glas je sintetiziran; stranica ne
sadrži snimljeni glas.

Stranica: https://same-same-ia.github.io/izlog/

## Japanske ljestvice

U izborniku Ljestvica, grupa "japanski", od najsvjetlije prema tamnijima. Sve su na D i imaju
po pet tonova. Nazivi su u romajiju (japanski latinicom).

- **hirajōshi II** i **hirajōshi I** — hirajōshi (平調子), "osnovni ugođaj": najčešći ugođaj
  kotoa, japanske citre. Zapadni priručnici iz njega izvode ljestvicu na više načina. I je
  čitanje Speeda te Kostke i Paynea (d e f a b), II je Burrowsovo (d fis gis a cis).
- **ryūkyū** (琉球音階) — okinavska ljestvica, iz narodne glazbe Okinave. Ryūkyū je staro ime
  otočja.
- **min'yō** (民謡音階) — ljestvica narodnih i dječjih pjesama.
- **ritsu II** — ritsu (律音階), ljestvica dvorske glazbe gagaku (雅楽). Literatura je čita na
  dva načina: d e g a h, što je isto kao yo, i d e g a c. ritsu II je drugo čitanje.
- **yo** (陽音階) — "svijetla" ljestvica. Stoji i u grupi "osnovni".
- **ryo** (呂音階) — drugi način dvorske glazbe, par ritsua.
- **kumoi** (雲井調子) — ugođaj kotoa "među oblacima".
- **miyako-bushi** (都節音階) — "gradski napjev", glazba gradova razdoblja Edo (1603–1868). Isto je što i in
  (陰音階), "sjenovita" ljestvica. Na njoj je pjesma sakura sakura (さくらさくら), "trešnjin cvijet".
- **in uzlazni** — in s c umjesto b, kako ga dio literature navodi za kretanje prema gore.
- **iwato** (岩戸調子) — ugođaj kotoa nazvan po kamenim vratima špilje u kojoj se, prema mitu,
  sakrila božica sunca.

Ryūkyū, min'yō, ritsu i miyako-bushi su četiri ljestvice iz sustava japanskog etnomuzikologa
Koizumija Fumija (小泉文夫).

## English

izlog is a hiragana board that sings: a tap plays the syllable sung at its own pitch, and
typed syllables become a short melody with its score. The voice is synthesized; the page
contains no recorded voice.

Page: https://same-same-ia.github.io/izlog/

**Privacy.** izlog collects nothing and sends nothing. It loads only its own files (code and
the voices' sound banks) from the address it was opened from. No analytics, no cookies, no
third-party scripts or fonts. On the device it remembers three settings only (theme, chosen
voice, View), and they never leave the device. After the first load the board works offline;
a voice not chosen before needs the network once.

**Japanese scales.** In the Scale picker, group "Japanese", from the brightest toward the
darker. All are on D with five tones; names are in romaji (Japanese in Latin letters).
hirajōshi (平調子), "standard tuning", the most common koto tuning, turned into a scale in
several ways by Western books: hirajōshi I follows Speed and Kostka & Payne (d e f a b),
hirajōshi II follows Burrows (d f♯ g♯ a c♯). ryūkyū (琉球音階): the Okinawan scale. min'yō (民謡音階): the scale of
folk and children's songs. ritsu (律音階): the scale of gagaku (雅楽) court music, read two ways
in the literature, d e g a b (the same as yo) and d e g a c; ritsu II is the second. yo
(陽音階): the "bright" scale, also in the basic group. ryo (呂音階): the other court-music mode,
ritsu's pair. kumoi (雲井調子): the koto tuning "among the clouds". miyako-bushi (都節音階):
"city song", the music of Edo-period cities; the same as in (陰音階), the "shaded" scale, and the
scale of the song sakura sakura (さくらさくら, "cherry blossom"). in ascending: in with c instead
of b, as part of the literature gives it for upward motion. iwato (岩戸調子): a koto tuning named
for the rock door of the cave where, in myth, the sun goddess hid. ryūkyū, min'yō, ritsu and
miyako-bushi are the four scales of the system of the Japanese ethnomusicologist Koizumi Fumio
(小泉文夫).
